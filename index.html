How to Use
  • Select belt type and channel (Z0-Z3 or A/B)
  • Position belt to pluck a 150mm section
  • Tap Measure and allow microphone access
  • Pluck the belt firmly with your fingernail
  • Hold phone 2-4 inches from the belt
  • Values update live on every pluck
  • Tap Stop to freeze the last reading
  • Measure all belts and check spread
